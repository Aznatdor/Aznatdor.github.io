About & Research Interests

I am a Master's student in Mathematics at Kyiv Academic University (joint program with the Institute of Mathematics of the NAS of Ukraine), advised by Georgii Riabov.

My research focuses on Functional Analysis, Optimal Transport Theory (Monge–Kantorovich and Beckmann formulations), Partial Differential Equations, and Infinite-Dimensional Probability.

Research & Manuscripts
